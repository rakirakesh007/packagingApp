# Requirements — Daily Sales Report

## Business Goal
Provide delivery boys with a summary of their daily sales for review and reconciliation.

## User Stories
- As a **delivery boy**, I want to see all my sales for today with totals.
- As a **delivery boy**, I want to filter by date to view past days.

## Numbered Requirements
1. Daily sales list grouped by shop
2. Date picker for historical view
3. Total amount and quantity summary
4. Export/share capability

## Key Files
- Frontend: `daily-sales/daily-sales.page.ts`
- Backend: `src/routes/reports.route.ts`
