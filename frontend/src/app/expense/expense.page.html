ℹ️ What to log here vs in Inventory (cost per sheet)

Rule: a cost that changes per pouch you make belongs in the item's cost per sheet (Inventory). A cost that does not (fuel, rent, phone…) belongs here as overhead.

These categories are treated as stock purchases — they do NOT reduce Net Profit (they're already inside cost per sheet):

  • Raw Material — the spice
  • Packaging — pouch + label
  • Salary — delivery-boy per-sheet charge

These are real overhead — they DO reduce Net Profit:

  • Fuel, Transport, Utilities, Maintenance, Miscellaneous, Other

How cost per sheet is calculated (in Inventory):

cost_per_sheet = material + labour + packing + delivery
material = pack_weight(g) × units_per_sheet × (rate₹/kg ÷ 1000) × 1.02

The 1.02 is 2% wastage. Do not add your margin — cost is only cost; margin = selling price − cost. This is the "Cost/sheet" column in your costing spreadsheet.
